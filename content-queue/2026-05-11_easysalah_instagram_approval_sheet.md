# EasySalah Instagram Approval Sheet

## What This Covers

This approval pack supports the existing 14-day EasySalah Instagram calendar in `content-queue/2026-05-10_easysalah_instagram_2weeks.json` and the matching Story concepts in `content-queue/2026-05-11_easysalah_instagram_2weeks_stories.json`.

## Campaign Direction

- Core message: salah brings clarity, steadiness, and mercy back into daily life.
- Brand angle: calm, privacy-minded, practical Islamic utility app with no invented features.
- Tone: warm elder-sibling guidance, encouraging without guilt or pressure.
- Visual direction: deep green, warm gold, off-white, subtle geometry, no faces, premium iPhone 15 Pro mockups where product appears.

## Content Mix Snapshot

- 14 feed posts total
- 4 educational posts
- 3 emotional posts

- 2 community posts

- 4 Reels, 5 Carousels, 5 Static posts
- 14 matching Stories, one per feed post

## Strongest Approval Points

- The calendar stays close to real EasySalah features: prayer reminders, adhan/tone controls, quiet hours, widgets, next-prayer view, privacy-minded simplicity, and on-device positioning.
- The Islamic language is gentle and respectful, with no shaming for missed prayer and no overclaiming on fiqh, precision, or endorsements.
- The schedule starts on the requested Monday and keeps the most engagement-led content weighted toward stronger audience days where possible.
- Story concepts are built to support the feed, not repeat it word-for-word, giving room for polls, sliders, quizzes, and question boxes.

## Review Notes Before Publishing
